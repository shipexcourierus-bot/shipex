FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question? We've got answers.

How do I track my package?

Simply visit our Tracking page and enter your unique tracking ID found in your shipment confirmation email or receipt. You'll see real-time updates including the current location, transit history, and estimated delivery time. No account required.

What are your delivery times?

We offer three service tiers: Express (1–2 business days), Standard (3–5 business days), and Economy (7–14 business days). International delivery times vary by destination and customs clearance. Estimated delivery dates are always shown on your tracking page.

Are my packages insured?

Yes! All shipments are automatically covered with basic insurance at no extra cost. For high-value items, we offer extended coverage. In the rare event of loss or damage, file a claim through our support team and receive compensation within 48 hours.

Do you ship internationally?

Absolutely. We currently serve 47+ countries across Africa, Europe, North America, and Asia. Our international team handles all customs documentation, duties, and clearances so you don't have to worry about cross-border complexities.

What happens if my package is delayed?

If your package is delayed beyond the estimated delivery date, our support team will proactively contact you. You can also reach us 24/7 via live chat on this page. We'll investigate, provide an updated timeline, and compensate you if the delay is on our end.

How do I become a business partner?

We love partnering with e-commerce businesses, retailers, and logistics platforms. Fill in the contact form below or email us at partnerships@shipex.com. Our business development team will reach out within 24 hours with a custom proposal tailored to your shipping volume.
